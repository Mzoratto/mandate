# Mandate public policy records

## Scope and mode

- Targets: `/privacy` and `/terms`
- Mode: Explain
- Audience: hackathon evaluators, repository users, and authenticated simulator operators
- Job: state exactly what the hosted demonstration processes, retains, permits, and does not claim
- Primary action: read or deep-link a policy section
- Constraint: public, tracker-free, no forms, no fabricated legal identity, and no implication of Alexa hosting or production readiness

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** Operational honesty extends to data handling and hosted-service limits.

**OWN-WORLD:** Use the established near-black field, steel rules, compact Inter, and one cyan channel point. State colors do not decorate policy text.

**STORY:** A plain-language title and effective date lead to an indexed reading column; each section maps directly to an implemented data or authority boundary.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** Public Mandate navigation, large policy title, concise scope statement, effective date, and the beginning of the ruled section index.

**FORM:** Editorial policy record with a sticky table of contents on desktop and a static two-column section map on mobile.

**FINISH:** complete only when both routes build statically, remain outside viewer authentication, link reciprocally from public/outcome-capture surfaces, pass desktop/mobile review, and produce zero automated axe violations

## Fixed decisions

- GitHub Issues is the public contact path; users are warned not to disclose sensitive data there.
- The policy names AWS, Neon, and GitHub as service providers.
- Retention language reflects the actual 14-day logs and minimum 30-day object lock.
- Terms distinguish the Apache-2.0 source license from hosted-service use.
