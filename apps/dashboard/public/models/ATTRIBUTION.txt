Portrait source: the Mandate visual reference supplied by the user.
The image is included unchanged as portrait-reference.png.
The browser samples only the portrait region into colored points.
Its labels and frame are excluded from the sampled region.
Frontal detail is fitted to an anatomical volume; unseen geometry is approximate.
